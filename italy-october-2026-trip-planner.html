Base in Chianti

Car · wineries · dining · markets

Da sapere

Practical notes

Book early

  • Vatican Museums & Colosseum: timed tickets online.
  • Uffizi Gallery: reservation required.
  • Car rental Firenze SMN: manual transmission by default.
  • Doge's Palace & gondola: a few days ahead.

On the ground

  • ZTL in Tuscany: fines are common, park outside the centres.
  • Dress code at basilicas: shoulders and knees covered.
  • Adapter type F/L, 230 V. A little cash is handy.
  • October weather: 15–23 °C, cool evenings.

Emergencies & useful

  • Emergencies (EU): 112
  • ETIAS may be required in 2026 — check before departure.
  • Passport valid 3 months past return.
