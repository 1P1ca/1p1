Base in Tavarnelle

Car · wineries · dining · markets
Rome → Tavarnelle drive (Oct 10) — taxi from Hotel La Rovere to the Roma Tiburtina depot (~€15–20). A1 north (~283 km, ~3h15) via the GRA, Firenze Impruneta exit — or the scenic SR222 Via Chiantigiana.
Tolls & fuel — take a ticket at the white lanes (avoid yellow Telepass), pay by card at the exit (~€18–22). Refuel at the "Self" pumps at Autogrill (~€40–50). ZTL: never drive into town centres. Return the car in Florence on Oct 17.
