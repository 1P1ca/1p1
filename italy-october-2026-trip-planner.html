Suggestion · at daybreak

Rome at dawn — the walk before the crowds

One morning, set the alarm early: at 6 AM Rome is almost empty. An outdoor loop of ≈ 6 km (~3.5 h) from the Colosseum to the Vatican — interiors closed, but the monuments seen without the crowds. Pairs naturally with the Vatican morning.

II

Chianti — Toscana

Oct 10 – 17 · 7 nights · Castello di Valle, Barberino Tavarnelle
Sat, Oct 10

Rome → Tuscany. Train Rome→Florence, pick up the car at Firenze SMN, drive into Chianti (≈ 45 min). Arrive at Castello di Valle from 3 PM. Dinner in San Donato in Poggio (Antica Trattoria La Toppa).

Sun, Oct 11

Wine road — Greve & Panzano. Tasting at Fontodi (Panzano, by reservation), lunch at Dario Cecchini. Vineyard views from Greve to Radda.

Mon, Oct 12

San Casciano market (morning), then Antinori nel Chianti Classico at Bargino: striking cellar-architecture, visit and tasting by reservation.

Tue, Oct 13

Florence (a weekday, less crowded). Uffizi, Duomo, Ponte Vecchio. Park at the Villa Costanza park-and-ride + tram to skip the ZTL.

Wed, Oct 14

Castellina in Chianti & Castello di Fonterutoli (Mazzei), the estate nearest the castle. Relaxed afternoon at the property.

Thu, Oct 15

Tavarnelle market (morning, the castle's own town), then Siena: Piazza del Campo and the cathedral. Park outside the walls.

Fri, Oct 16

San Gimignano and its towers, a final tasting (Rocca delle Macìe), a farewell terroir dinner and packing. Option: Val d'Orcia — Montalcino, Pienza.

III

Venezia

Oct 17 – 20 · 3 nights · Dimora Capuleti · Return VCE → YUL

Base in Chianti

Car · wineries · dining · markets
Car: pick up at Firenze SMN (station) when the train arrives on Oct 10, return to the same point on Oct 17 before the train to Venice — round trip in Florence, no one-way fee. A compact (Fiat 500 / Panda) for the narrow roads, or a small SUV for comfort. Sixt, in the underground garage (level -4), sits outside the ZTL — the easiest way out. Never drive into the historic centres (Florence, Siena, San Gimignano): park on the outskirts. ≈ 45 min from the station to the castle.

Wineries — book ahead

  • Castello di Fonterutoli (Mazzei), Castellina — nearest the castle book
  • Fontodi, Panzano — free tasting, tour by reservation book
  • Antinori nel Chianti Classico, Bargino — cellar-architecture book
  • Castello di Ama, Gaiole — wine & contemporary art book

  • Locanda di Pietracupa, Castellina — refined table
  • Dario Cecchini / Officina della Bistecca, Panzano — the bistecca book

Da sapere

Practical notes

Book early

  • Vatican Museums and Colosseum: timed tickets online, often sold out on site.
  • Uffizi Gallery (Florence): a reservation is effectively required.
  • Car rental Florence: pick-up/drop-off at the station, manual transmission by default.
  • Doge's Palace and the gondola: book a few days ahead.

Logistics & good to know

  • ZTL (limited-traffic zones) in Tuscany: fines are common, park outside the centres.
  • ETIAS authorization may be required for the EU in 2026 — check before departure.
  • Adapter type F/L, 230 V. Cards accepted everywhere, a little cash is handy.
  • October weather: 15–23 °C, cool evenings — bring layers.
