            Rome

            Ancient sights · dawn walk · dining
            Book ahead: the Vatican Museums, Sistine Chapel and Colosseum sell out routinely — buy timed tickets online well before Oct 7. St Peter's Basilica itself is free to enter but requires a security line.

            Base in Tavarnelle

            Car · wineries · dining · markets
            Rome → Tavarnelle drive (Oct 10) — taxi from Hotel La Rovere to the Roma Tiburtina depot (~€15–20). A1 north (~283 km, ~3h15) via the GRA, Firenze Impruneta exit — or the scenic SR222 Via Chiantigiana.
            Tolls & fuel — take a ticket at the white lanes (avoid yellow Telepass), pay by card at the exit (~€18–22). Refuel at the "Self" pumps at Autogrill (~€40–50). ZTL: never drive into town centres. Return the car in Florence on Oct 17.

            Venice

            On foot & by vaporetto · lagoon islands
            Getting around: Venice has no cars — everything moves on foot or by vaporetto (water bus). If island-hopping to Murano, Burano or Torcello, a 48h or 72h ACTV travel pass is usually cheaper than single tickets.

            Short on time · on foot

            • Doge's Palace book
            • St Mark's Basilica
            • Rialto Bridge
            • San Polo district
            • Gondola

            Lagoon islands

            • Torcello — small, historical, peaceful
            • Mazzorbo — quiet, vineyard
            • San Michele — the cemetery island
            • Lido — beach; Film Festival in September
            • Correr, La Fenice, San Giorgio Maggiore — with 3 days

            Book early

            • Vatican Museums & Colosseum — timed tickets online
            • Uffizi Gallery — reservation required
            • Car rental Roma Tiburtina — manual transmission by default
            • Doge's Palace & gondola — a few days ahead

            On the ground

            • ZTL in Tuscany — fines are common, park outside the centres
            • Dress code at basilicas — shoulders and knees covered
            • Adapter type F/L, 230 V; a little cash is handy
            • October weather — 15–23 °C, cool evenings

            Emergencies & useful

            • Emergencies (EU) — 112
            • ETIAS may be required in 2026 — check before departure
            • Passport valid 3 months past return
